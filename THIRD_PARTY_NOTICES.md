## Felt Backgrounds (green) (game board background texture)

- Asset: Felt Backgrounds (green)

- Source: OpenGameArt
- URL: https://opengameart.org/content/felt-backgrounds
- License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
- Modification: Recolored to #0E7A44 and compressed for use as the game-board background

Only the green image (`felt_green.jpg`) from the official `felt.tar.gz`
download was used; it was recolored (base tone `#0E7A44`, with the
original's uneven lighting/blotches flattened while keeping its fine
felt-fiber grain) and re-exported as `src/assets/textures/board-felt.webp`.
The brown/grey/pink variants and the original archive are not included in
this repository.
